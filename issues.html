Affordability

Mike has spent 40 years watching North Fulton grow and the cost of living grow right along with it. Rising property taxes, skyrocketing home prices, and an avalanche of regulations are squeezing working families and pushing small businesses out. Mike knows how Georgia’s economy works from the ground up, and he knows the solution is not more government, it is less. He will fight to cut taxes, streamline regulations, and get bureaucracy out of the way so North Fulton families can thrive in the community they have built.

Fiscal Responsibility

Before Mike votes to spend your tax dollars, he will ask one question: would the hardest working people in Georgia, the roofers, the construction workers, the small business owners getting up before dawn, would they sign off on this? Because it is their money! Mike has built a career on helping companies make smart decisions to prosper and deliver real results. He will bring that same discipline to the Georgia General Assembly and fight to make sure your tax dollars are spent wisely. Then, Mike will work to cut the state income tax and property taxes, so Georgians will be able to keep more of their hard earned money.



Education

North Fulton is home to some of the finest schools in the country and Mike intends to keep it that way. But for too long, public education has operated as a monopoly and it is time to turn that around. Mike will fight for real school choice, where state education dollars follow the child, so every family has the freedom to choose the right school, public, private, charter or home school. At our colleges and universities, tuition costs have skyrocketed while accountability has lagged behind. Mike will push for merit based hiring, transparent curriculum, and real spending justification from Georgia’s colleges and universities. A degree from a school in Georgia should be an investment in your future, that you can build a career on. Not a system that takes your money and doesn’t have to justify how it spends it.

Public Safety

North Fulton families deserve to feel safe in their neighborhoods, their schools, and their businesses. Mike will stand fully behind Georgia’s law enforcement and first responders, not just in words, but in policy. He will push for stronger interagency cooperation, including full cooperation with ICE to apprehend criminal illegal aliens who have no business being in our communities. Cities and counties that refuse to cooperate should face real penalties; sanctuary policies have no place in Georgia. Mike will fight to make sure that when criminals are arrested and convicted, they face real consequences. No revolving doors. No soft-on-crime politics. Just safe streets and a justice system that actually works for the people it is supposed to protect.
